## Direction contract

THESIS: Riffle’s shell is a daylit learning network — navigation stays familiar, and the roadmap is a bright corridor map of stations (lessons) and lines (genres/paths) so “what’s next” is always a visible stop, not a buried card stack.

OWN-WORLD: Cool daylight ground (#F0F4F8), deep navy ink (#0B1F33), cyan route (#0E7490), amber (#CA8A04) and green (#15803D) as path/state inks only; humanist transit-clean sans; porcelain-white panels; density of a wayfinding board without enamel costume. Signature move: the active learning corridor diagram with interchange states (open / active / complete / locked).

STORY: Visitor lands on Overview, sees today’s station and Resume Practice immediately, understands the wider roadmap as connected lines, then drills into Practice, Library, or Preferences through standard nav.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail with Riffle mark + Overview / Preferences / Roadmap / Library / Practice Room; main pane led by corridor map of Acoustic Pop (and other) lines with next station “Alternate Picking Mastery” and primary Resume Practice; secondary strip for progress stats and recent activity — no marketing hero, no ticket/departures costume.

FORM: Bright Corridor Map (daylit transit-diagram grammar) — catalog challenger wayfinding-cartography-signage-midnight-transit-diagram translated to bright Operate shell; seed key 04061e4a.
